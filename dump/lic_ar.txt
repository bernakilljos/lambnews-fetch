1. APPLICATION 

1.1  This is an application for the granting of a Premises Licence (variation application) 

under  Section  34  of  the Licensing Act 2003. 

1.2  The applicant is seeking authorisation to  vary the extant premises licence to add late 

night  refreshment; extend  the venues hours of operation (effectively so that the venue 

can carry on licensable activity all day across the whole week)  and  to amend condi tions 

8 and 20 of the premises licence , as detailed below .

1.3 A copy of the application form is attached to the report as Annex A.

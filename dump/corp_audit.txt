## RECOMMENDATIONS 

1. To consider progress made against the management actions arising from limited internal audit 

assurance reports and the level of assurance received. 1. CONTEXT 

1.1 The Accounts and Audit Regulations 2015 state that a relevant authority must ensure that it has a 

sound system of internal control which :

 facilitates the effective exercise of its functions and the achievement of its aims and 

objectives; 

 ensures that the financial and operational management of the authority is effective; and 

 includes effective arrangements for the management of risk. 

1.2 The Corporate Committee has responsibility for oversight of the Council’s arrangements for 

governance, risk management and control and this report assists the Com mittee in discharging its 

responsibilities. 

## 2. PROPOSAL AND REASONS 

2.1 The Corporate Committee, in its ‘audit committee’ role, receives regular reports on the outcomes of 

internal audit activity in sufficient detail to allow the Committee to understand what as surance it can 

take from that work and/or what unresolved risks or issues it needs to address in respect of 

governance, risk management and internal control arrangements. 

2.2 This report provides an update from management on the actions arising from internal audit s that 

were previously reported to the Committee and offered limited assurance : Absence Management 

(see appendix A). Progress has been positive with actions either delivered or on track for delivery 

within agreed time frame. The Resources Directorate Leadership Team are monitoring to 

completion. 

2.3 Internal Audit will follow up all medium and high priority actions and seek evidence to confirm their 

implementation. The internal audit report in January summarise s all overdue and high -risk actions 

that are incomplete. 

## 3. FINANCE 

3.1 This report does not give rise to any additional capital or revenue financial implications. 

## 4. LEGAL AND DEMOCRACY 

4.1 The provision of Internal Audit is a statutory requirement for all local authorities that is 

set out in the Accounts and Audit Regulations 2015. These state that a relevant authority must 

undertake an effective internal audit to evaluate the effectiveness of its risk management, control 

and governance processes, taking into account public sector i nternal auditing standards and 

guidance. 

4.2 Internal Audit also has a role in helping the Council to fulfil its responsibilities under 

s.151 of the Local Government Act 1972, which specify that each local authority shall make 

arrangements for the proper admi nistration of their financial affairs and shall secure that one of 

their officers has responsibility for the administration of those affairs. 

4.3 There are no further comments from Democratic Services.

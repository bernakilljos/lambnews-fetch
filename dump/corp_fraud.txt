## 2. PROPOSAL AND REASONS 

2.1 The Corporate Committee, in its ‘audit committee’ role, receives regular reports on the progress 

and outcomes of counter fraud activity and investigation work . The Committee should consider 

what assurance can be derived and what , if any, unresolved risks o r issues it needs to address. 

2.2 In keeping with the recommendations made by the Chartered Institute of Public Finance and 

Accountancy (CIPFA ), counter fraud activity is reported separately to internal audit. This report 

provides an update on counter fraud activity for the 202 5/26 financial year (to 31 December 2025 ).

Counter Fraud Progress 2025/26 

2.3 Appendix One shows progress to date in key areas of activity. Briefly: 

a) New investigations are at a higher rate than in the previous year although this can change 

month by month and doesn’t reflect any issue or concern (chart one) .

b) Active investigations are up on last year’s totals due to a higher rate of new investigations 

and fewer closures (chart two) . The reduction in the number of closures isn’t significant and 

may increase in the coming months in line with the previous year’s total 

c) Tenancy recommendations are up on the previous year . In addition, the number of 

outstanding recommendations with Housing and Legal currently stands at 164 compared to 

148 in April 20 25 . This is reflected in the number of recoveries which is down on last year 

(charts three and four) . Although recoveries are lower at this stage than in the previous 

year, we are hopeful that more recoveries will be secured by Housing and Legal because of 

the recommendations we have made . The process of property recovery is not always 

linear, and Counter Fraud are not in control of how quickly properties are recovered. If more 

tenants are fighting recovery proceedings rather than relinquishing their tenanc ies when 

found to be committing fraud this delays the time taken to get properties back. 

d) Internal investigation reports are higher than last year des pite being one internal 

investigator down since May 2025 (chart s even ).

2.4 Fraud p revention reviews are carried out in business areas with functions that are at a high -risk of 

fraud. Our Fraud Prevention Officer will work with the services concerned to highlight the risks and 

to suggest how he risks can be mitigated. 

2.5 A fraud prevention review has recently been completed relating to Homelessness and Temporary 

Accommodation. The review and recommendations ha ve been shared with management, and we 

are awaiting their responses. Other reviews are in progress , includi ng Contract Management . Th e

objective of the review s is to ascertain the current process and procedures in place and what 

action is taken in relation to fraud risks in th ese area s. The reviews will result in report s to 

management and will include recommendations of what action can be taken to minimise the risk of 

fraud . The recommendations and resulting management actions will be tracked through to 

completion. 

2.6 The Fraud Prevention Officer is rolling out counter -fraud training to Contract Managers and those 

involved in the procureme nt life cycle. Discussions are ongoing with Procurement to include this in 

their training schedule, and it is anticipated that the training will take place before the end of the 

current financial year. 

2.7 The Lambeth and Croydon Counter Fraud Shared Service are members of the Government 

Counter Fraud Profession (GC FP) through the Local Authority Collective who hold self -certifying membership . Counter Fraud are participating in peer review s which are a requirement of 

membership . Peer reviews provide assurance to the GCFP tha t members of the collective are 

meeting the required standards . The Hertfordshire Anti -Fraud Shared Service recently completed a 

review o f the Counter Fraud Service and concluded that , ‘the Counter Fraud Team at Lambeth 

Council is long established and well run, providing an excellent service for both Lambeth and 

Croydon councils. The C ounter Fraud Team is a recognised centre of excellence with its officers 

receiving national awards, its support and leadership of the local authorit y collective for the 

Government Counter Fraud Profession and its work with L ondon Boroughs Fraud Investigators 

Group and other C ounter Fraud networks across the public sector. 

2.8 Two recommendations were made as part of the review both of which have been acce pted and 

being implemented . Both documents will be finalised for 01/04/2026 ; the Strategy will be shared at 

the March Corporate Committee meeting. The Manual is an operational document and as such 

isn’t shared with Committee. However, if Members would like sight of the document, it can be 

shared outside of Committee. 

 The Counter Fraud Manual that provides a step -by -step approach to a wide variety of 

operational work. This document is being updated at present and although it i s shared with 

the whole team when published as the team changes or officers change roles within the 

Team they may not always be aware of this document. If officers could be reminded of the 

manual at team meetings or annual reviews and provided with a copy/ link when they join 

the service , this might help to keep sight of it and ensure a consistent approach to all work 

across the service and compliance with legislation and local policies. 

 The C ounter Fraud Policy and Response Plan appear to replicate the much more detailed 

Counter Fraud Strategy. The recommendation was to combine the two policy documents 

with the new Strategy Document to provide once over -arching source and prevent 

duplication.

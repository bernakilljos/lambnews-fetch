Where a valid and reasonable request for a licence review is made to the Authority, the 

Authority will initiate a process to consider and undertake a review. 

The licensing sub -committee, in determining a review, may exercise the range of powers 

given to them to promote the licensing objectives. Any steps taken must be appropriate 

and proportionate in prom oting the licensing objectives. The Authority will seek to establish the cause or causes of the concern, and remedial 

action will be targeted at such causes. Proceedings under the Licensing Act 2003 for 

reviewing a premises licence are provided as a key p rotection for the community where 

problems associated with crime and disorder, public safety, public nuisance, or the 

protection of children from harm occur. 

1. APPLICATION 

1.1 On 10 June 2025 an application to review the premises licence under Section 51 of 

the Licensing Act 2003 was submitted  by the Metropolitan Police  in respect  to

Aquarium Tapas Bar 415 Wandsworth Road  London  SW8 2JP .

1.2  The  applica tion  cites  concern  that the Premises Licence  Holder has failed to 

promote the licensing objectives of the prevention of  crime and disorder ,

prevention of public nuisance  and p ublic  safety. 

1.3 On 26 February 2023, s everal patrons at the premises were involved in an 

argument . When as one of those patrons attempt ed to leave the premises , that 

person was allegedly threatened by another patron with a firearm. This incident 

occurred at 05:20 hours which was beyond the p ermitted operating hours for 

the premises of 04 :00 Hours . Following this incident, t he designated premises 

supervisor , Jorge De Freitas was invited to a licensing intervention meeting with 

officers from the Metropolitan Police and the Council’s Licensing team , where it 

was agreed that a voluntary variation application would be submitted to r educe 

the premises terminal hour to 02 :00 hours . The promised application was not 

submitted. 

1.3. On 2 March 2025 at 06:05 hours , police officers attended the premises to 

investi gat e an alleged assault . The officers spoke to Jorge De Freitas who 

confirmed that an altercation had taken place at the premises and admitted that 

the venue was op en and op erating beyond its permitted hours. 

1.4. On 1 April 2025, the Police Licensing team had a meeting with Mr De Freitas 

where he a dmitted to several breaches of conditions on the premises licence 

and that he sometimes finds it difficult to get patrons to leave the premises at 

the stipulated terminal hour on the licence .

1.5. A copy of the review application is attached to the report as Annex A. 

2. RE PRESENTATION 

2.1. One representation ha s been received in support of the review application from the 

Licensing Authority based on the licensing objectives of the prevention of crime and 

disorder , prevention of public nuisance and public safety. 

2.2. The Licensing Authority has no confidence in the management or operation of the 

premises following several breaches and the failure of the designated premises 

supervisor to promote the licensing objectives. A copy of the representation w as 

provided to the licence holder and is attached to the report as Annex B.

2.3. Two representations have been received in support of the premises . One from a 

member of the public and the other is a petition signed by eight individuals . The 

representations stated that they have no concerns about the operation of the 

premises and the premises have no adverse impact on the area . Copies of the representation s were provided to the licence holder and are attached to the report 

as Annex C.

2.4. The last date for making representations was 8 July 2025 .

The options available to Members of the Licensing Sub -Committee are t o: 

1) To take no action. 

2) To modify the conditions of the licence (and for this purpose, the conditions of 

the licence are modified if any of them are altered or omitted, or any new 

conditions are added. This may include alteration to the permitted hours ). 

3) To exclude a licensable activity from the scope of the premises licence. 

4) To remove the designated premises supervisor from the licence. 

5) To suspend the premises licence for a period not exceeding 3 months. 

6) To revoke the premises licence. 

Should the sub -committee decide to take either step 2) or 3) in paragraph 6.1 above, 

the modification or exclusion can be permanently or for a specified period only (not 

exceeding three months).

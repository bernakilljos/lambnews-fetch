# REPORT SUMMARY 

The Council is on a path to financial sustainability and this report sets out the progress made so far in 

this financial year to deliver a sustainable balanced budget and Medium Term Financial Strategy 

(MTFS ). The p osition reflects the significant and on -going pressure on the Council’s finances because of 

the real terms cuts in government funding since 2010. In that time, the Council’s funding has reduced by 

more than 28%, while demand for our services has increased and the inflation rate has been at a 

generational high. 

The Council now has around £116m a year less to spend on local services compared to if government 

funding reductions had not taken place . Whilst in recent years we have seen increases in core spendin g

power, these do not bring us back to former funding levels in real terms and neither have these kept 

pace with increases in the demand for our services. As a result of the housing crisis, demand for 

temporary accommodation has reached unprecedented levels across London and taken up increasing 

amounts of London boroughs’ budgets. We also continue to face demand pressures in the key areas of 

adults and childre n’s social care. The Council’s financial planning has also been frustrated by a series of 

one -year settlements in previous years . T he current government’s commitment to multi -year settlements 

is welcome.

# 1. CONTEXT 1.1 The Council is required by statute to set a balanced budget each year. The Council undertakes i ts

financial planning through the MTFS process wh ich is a four year planning period . The MTFS 

estimates expected resources and spending requirements over the planning period and w here a 

funding gap exists between expected income and expenditure, savings proposals are developed to 

balance the financial position. 

1.2 The main objectives of our MTFS are to: 

 Prioritise our resources in -line with Our Future, Our Borough: Lambeth 2030, do so in line 

with our commitment to be a Borough of Equity and Justice and to ensure we achieve our 

2030 ambitions: 

o Making Lambeth neighbourhoods fit for the future. 

o Making Lam beth one of the safest boroughs in London. 

o Making Lambeth a place we can all call home. 

 Maintain a balanced budget position, and always to set an MTFS which maintains and 

strengthens that position. 

 Provide a robust framework to assist the decision -makin g process within the Council. 

 Manage the council’s finances with a forward looking four year rolling strategy. 

 Deliver value for money for our residents and businesses .

 Exercise probity, prudence, and strong financial control. 

 Manage risk, which includ es holding reserves and balances at an appropriate and 

sustainable level. 

 Continually review budgets to ensure resources are targeted on our key priorities. 

# 2. PROPOSAL AND REASONS 

2.1 In July we published an updated Medium Term Financial Strategy (MTFS) which estimated a 

budget gap of £84m across the 2026/27 to 2029/30 period. To meet the statutory requirement to 

deliver a balanced budget, si nce the publication of the revised MTFS, work has accelerated to 

close the estimated budget gap through the development of budget proposals. The process has 

included: 

 Several rounds of budget challenge sessions with Cabinet Members, Corporate Directors an d the 

Corporate Director of Resources and Cabinet Member Finance .

 Review of benchmarking data and a line -by -line review of budgets. 

 The development of budget proposals which have been subject to review and challenge by 

Informal Cabinet. 

2.2 The second tranche of savings proposals have now been reviewed by the Equality and Justice Panel 

and are recommended for approval by Cabinet to contribute to closing the MTFS gap. The proposals 

are contained in the report to be considered by Cabinet on 2 February 20 26 which is in the attached 

Appendix .

2.3 The overall budget position for 2026/2 7, and the MTFS period, will be considered at the Cabinet 

meeting of 23 February and the Council meeting of 4 March 2026 . This will bring together the 

savings approved by Cabinet , at the meetings in November and February, other budget changes 

and expected resources over the MTFS period .

# 3. FINANCE 

3.1 This report concerns the Council’s financial position and actions being taken to ensure a balanced 

budget is delivered for 2026/27 and the financial planning period. 4. LEGAL AND DEMOCRACY 

4.1 The Council has a duty to maintain a balanced budget throughout the year and, accordingly, 

members are required to regularly monitor the Council's financial position. In implementing the 

Council’s financial strategy for 202 6/2 7 and subsequent years, members will need to balance the 

proposed level of expenditure in discretionary areas of service provision against that required to 

ensure that the Council complies with its statutory duties. 

4.2. Section 28 of the Local Government Act 2003 imposes a duty on the Council to monitor its budgets 

throughout the financial year, using the same figures fo r reserves as were used in the original 

budget calculations. The Council must take necessary appropriate action to deal with any 

deterioration in the financial position revealed by the review. 

4. 3. In reaching decisions on these matters, members are bou nd by the general principles of 

administrative law. Lawful discretions must not be abused or fettered, and all relevant 

considerations must be taken into account. No irrelevant considerations may be taken into 

account, and any decision made must not be suc h that no reasonable authority, properly directing 

itself, could have reached. Members must also balance the interests of service users against those 

who contribute to the Council’s finances. Monies may not be expended thriftlessly and the full 

resources a vailable to the Council must be deployed to their best advantage. Members must also 

act prudently and in a business -like manner at all times. 

4. 4. In considering the advice of officers, and the weight to be attached to that advice, members should 

have r egard to the personal duties placed upon the Corporate Director of Resources as Chief 

Financial Officer. The Chief Financial Officer is required by Section 151 of the Local Government 

Act 1972 and by the Accounts and Audit Regulations 2015 to ensure that t he Council’s budgeting, 

financial management, and accounting practices meet relevant statutory and professional 

requirements. In the event that the Council’s overall financial position worsens considerably during 

the remainder of 202 5/2 6, the Corporate Dir ector of Resources will need to have regard to the 

statutory obligations which are placed on her personally when deciding on any particular actions to 

be recommended to Members to address her concerns. There are no specific legal implications 

arising out o f this report.
